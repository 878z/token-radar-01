A clear-eyed view of protocols building before a token.

Each protocol's implied value is its TVL times the median market-cap-to-TVL multiple of already-tokenized protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the global median for small categories. We adjust that anchor with a bounded momentum factor from 7-day TVL change, a saturating seasoning factor from how long DefiLlama has tracked it, a mild log discount for very large TVL, and a small multi-chain breadth bonus.
